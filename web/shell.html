raylib Quake

id Software's 1996 Quake engine (the WinQuake software renderer, released under the GPL) running on raylib and WebAssembly, with the free shareware episode Dimension of the Doomed.

Touch: drag on the left half of the screen to move (the further, the faster), drag on the right half to look around. FIRE, JUMP and WPN › (next weapon) are on the right, MENU (Esc) at the top: New Game, Load and Save are in Single Player. In menus tap the picture for Enter, swipe or use the arrows to move, Y/N answer questions. Play sideways. ?touch=0 in the address turns the touch controls off, ?touch=1 forces them on, and ?console=1 adds a console button (~) with the phone's keyboard.

Settings (config.cfg) and saved games stay in this browser (IndexedDB). There is no multiplayer in the browser build: a page cannot open UDP sockets. Starting a new game from the menu while one is running does not ask for confirmation here. After quitting, reload the page to play again. Extra command-line options: ?args=-skill%202%20%2Bmap%20e1m2.
